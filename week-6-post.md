### [Survival Of The Best Fit]

**Navigate to the website, [https://www.survivalofthebestfit.com/](https://www.survivalofthebestfit.com/) and click on the button at the top of the page, “Start Game”. Complete the game**

**What was the motivating factor or factors during the game which resulted in the integration of a Machine Learning model to evaluate job candidates through automated resume review?**
- The need to hire more candidates faster since hiring candidates manually went too slow
- Needing to hire faster for more funding for the startup

**What “good” situations existed in the game?**
- A variety of candidates with varying levels of Skill, School Prestige, Work Experience and Ambition
- Startup is growing so employees are needed

**What “bad” situations existed in the game?**
- Having more orange than blue candidates when selecting them for training the model
- Do not know what kind of candidates the big tech companies hired in their huge applicant records when comparing it to our data and if they valued certain attributes
- Only CVs were used to make the hiring decisions instead of considering other factors
- After automated the hiring process and when Elvan Yang is wrongfully denied, most of the rejected candidates are blue most likely due to having more orange candidates initially
- Ambition seems to be the attribute that gets more candidates hired in the post hiring automation process.
  - Initially after starting the game, says how much more school prestige has than average candidates, so focused on school prestige being important. As a result after being automated, more candidates were rejected than accepted and startup did not work
  - However, when reading through the scrollbar after automated, saw that it mentions ambition as being important. I tested this by selecting candidates with higher ambition. This led to more candidates getting accepted than rejected

**How would you advise the business leader about when and how to implement a Machine Learning model to automate or improve the throughput of a hiring process?**
- I would advise them against the implementation of the Machine Learning model since there are biases that exist even if they are not intended
- For the game, initially I was looking at the candidate’s attributes and did not realize that there were more orange candidates shown to me compared to blue candidates.
